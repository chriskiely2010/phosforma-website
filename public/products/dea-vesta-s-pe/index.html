Overview

Dea Vesta S PE is the compact Ø60mm version of the Vesta pendant, on a surface-mounted ceiling cup that installs on any ceiling. Use it alone over a side table or counter, or in clusters, with a black or white body and seven reflector finishes. A dim-to-warm version suits more intimate spaces.
